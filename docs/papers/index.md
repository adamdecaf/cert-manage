# Papers

Research on CA trust stores that motivates trustprune. See [Why](../why.md) for context.

- [Analysis of the HTTPS Certificate Ecosystem](https-imc13.pdf) (2013) — more than **1,800 entities** able to issue certificates for any website; compromise of one intermediate would force ~26% of HTTPS sites to re-issue.
- [CAge — Taming Certificate Authorities by Inferring Restricted Scopes](cage-fc13.pdf) — simple inference rules cut attack surface nearly tenfold without blocking most CA activity.
- [You Won't Be Needing These Any More: On Removing Unused Certificates From Trust Stores](on-removing-unused-certs.pdf) — only about two-thirds of trusted roots were observed signing HTTPS certificates.
